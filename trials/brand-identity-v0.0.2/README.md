# BART Brand Identity Template v0.0.2 trial

This trial copies the [released kit](https://github.com/vixttha/Brand-Identity-Template/releases/tag/v0.0.2) at [`87d0f9aedbe69ca4b16815d6b3329efcf5889da9`](https://github.com/vixttha/Brand-Identity-Template/commit/87d0f9aedbe69ca4b16815d6b3329efcf5889da9). BART adaptations inside the copy are the project brief, creative decision and empty project inventory. The existing [Facet drafts](https://github.com/vixttha/Blender-Agent-Refinement-Toolkit/tree/9741c63beaa237ab6fcd3672e2dff5e49e759b5b/brand-identity) are the historical comparison.

## How agent identity became brand identity

1. [The naming decision](https://github.com/vixttha/Blender-Agent-Refinement-Toolkit/issues/62) distinguishes **Blender-Agent Refinement Toolkit**, the toolkit people install, from **BART**, the agent they refer to when using it. “Give shape to an idea” expresses the product promise.
2. [The public rebrand](https://github.com/vixttha/Blender-Agent-Refinement-Toolkit/pull/67) put those names into the public README and active documentation. [Instructions HQ](https://github.com/vixttha/Blender-Agent-Refinement-Toolkit/pull/73) records the same distinction in project instructions. **Archivewright** is the instructions stewardship role.
3. [The visual branch](https://github.com/vixttha/Blender-Agent-Refinement-Toolkit/tree/9741c63beaa237ab6fcd3672e2dff5e49e759b5b/brand-identity) gave the name a Facet logo, wordmark, palette and application examples. The trial asks how well those choices communicate what BART does on a small GitHub avatar and in the product documentation.

## Creative review

The first placement is a **32 px circular GitHub avatar**, supported by the full product name in live text. Try the mark in one color in both themes. Judge the opening and silhouette at 16 px too, where failure is more likely. The source idea is the product's ability to read and refine geometry, as shown by the triangle inspection walkthrough and the separate output paths. The meanings below are design interpretations of the [walkthrough](https://github.com/vixttha/Blender-Agent-Refinement-Toolkit/blob/3e7f9e02e61739825b56bcafecebbd0221cc9657/README.md), [architecture](https://github.com/vixttha/Blender-Agent-Refinement-Toolkit/blob/3e7f9e02e61739825b56bcafecebbd0221cc9657/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md), and [naming decision](https://github.com/vixttha/Blender-Agent-Refinement-Toolkit/issues/62).

### Project name

**Blender-Agent Refinement Toolkit** is the locked product name. **Blender-Agent** describes the agent's Blender workflow. **Refinement** describes the work of inspecting and improving geometry. **Toolkit** sets the expectation of several practical operations and entry points. **BART** is the locked agent name and the proposed uppercase text for the drawn wordmark.

### Logo shortlist

Lead category is an **abstract mark**. Lead art style is a flat technical drawing with a heavy, one-color silhouette and one legible opening. A lettermark also fits the avatar. A mascot would put the character ahead of the geometry use case.

| Draft direction | Product detail | Audience reading | Project principle | Trade-off | Score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Section Line | Two solid stepped profiles suggest a cut through a 3D form. | A wide opening makes the shape readable without color. | The complete profiles on both sides refer to inspecting and refining while keeping a source intact. | The opening could close visually at 16 px. | 90 |
| Mesh Joint | Two counters of a compact B suggest adjoining mesh faces. | The B gives readers an immediate cue for BART. | A single deliberate seam refers to a precise, localized edit. | The joint could disappear or make the B look broken. | 87 |
| Reference Frame | An open frame around a stepped form suggests measured bounds. | Viewers can judge how the object sits in its workspace. | A frame that leaves one side open suggests room for iteration. | The mark may read as a crop icon. | 78 |

The released rubric weights brand fit 35%, artistic character 25%, distinctiveness 25%, and versatility 15%. These are written-concept ratings. Small-size readability and resemblance to existing marks need an artwork review.

### Slogan and supporting copy

| Line | Product meaning | Audience meaning | Principle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Give shape to an idea.** | “Shape” connects the line to geometry. | “Give” leaves agency with the person using BART. | “Idea” leaves room for iteration before the result is final. |
| **Inspect, refine, and convert 3D assets locally.** | Names three documented operations. | Helps GitHub visitors recognize the use case immediately. | “Locally” reflects the documented execution model. |

The first is the existing brand line. The second is a proposed supporting descriptor for a surface where readers need the utility explained.

### Wordmark shortlist

Both treatments read exactly **BART**.

| Treatment | Product detail | Audience reading | Project principle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Open square-cut capitals** | Repeat the logo's opening in the A counter or diagonal R leg. | Give A and R enough space to stay distinct on a narrow header. | Use one cut detail repeatedly to express a precise, inspectable edit. |
| **Rounded technical capitals** | Use short curves and straight joins to echo a refined edge. | Softer corners offer a calmer reading in documentation. | Surviving on one ink and a simple outline keeps the work adaptable. |

**Proposed pairing:** Section Line and open square-cut BART capitals, with the existing slogan in live text. The pairing is open for feedback. Artwork preflight follows confirmation of the exact wording. The [decision record](brand-identity/01-strategy/creative-decision.json) retains an empty confirmation field.

This review uses the [meaning-led method](https://github.com/vixttha/Brand-Identity-Template/blob/fff8e5daba8defe4af8cac5bb21f980e3616e5fb/brand-identity/01-strategy/meaning-method.md) as a design exercise and keeps the released kit pinned to v0.0.2.
